03 · Process

From discarded
weight to living
archive.

Four steps transform a second-hand garment into a tracked, traceable object with a scannable identity that outlasts the tag.

i
Step 01

Source
by weight

Garments sourced from certified surplus warehouses at €2–4 /kg. Each piece inspected for structural integrity; distressed fabric accepted at lower grade.

ii
Step 02

Design the
medallion

Customer describes a motif. Claude structures it into a 7-parameter JSON spec. A deterministic SVG renderer builds the codec medallion in real time.

iii
Step 03

Print on
J850

PolyJet J850. 30 mm tile, ~1.2 g material. Reed-Solomon parity ensures the codec survives 40 % surface wear before read degrades.

iv
Step 04

QR passport
activated

First scan activates the garment's digital passport: provenance chain, repair log, ownership history. Immutable, open, linkable to resale platforms.

Hand-drawn motif

Sketch a shape — a leaf, a sigil, a crest. The atelier embeds your 32-bit key into the drawing as subtle micro-modulations in stroke height, invisible to the eye but readable by the codec.

How it works

Your strokes become the silhouette. The codec adds 32 imperceptible relief markers around the boundary — one per parity bit — and modulates stroke height by the channel signature. The drawing is yours; the key is hidden in the relief.

Order workflow

From a freshly minted item ID to a sewn-on, scannable tile — the path through the atelier, the print service, and your activation step. Click each stage to advance.

01

Mint identifier

The atelier issues a 32-bit ID and bound metadata: maker, collection, garment, material.

Ready · 0x2DCA3791
02

Generate STL

Channels encode into a 30 mm tile. Reed-Solomon parity printed as 32 rim markers. Single .stl file, ~600 KB.

Awaiting
03

Print & receive

Send .stl to atelier print service or local printer. Tile arrives as a flexible disc, ready to stitch.

Awaiting
04

Activate

Sew to the garment. Open the scanner. First successful read activates the record on the registry — provenance is live.

Awaiting

Atelier dispatch

An identifier has been minted. When you're ready, generate the STL, send it to the print service, and stitch the resulting tile to your garment. The first scan activates the registry entry — and from then on, the pattern is the provenance.
